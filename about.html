Hobby photographer based in Gaspésie, Québec.

Alexandre Perron — Gaspésie, Québec

I'm a photographer based in Gaspésie, Quebec, on the shore of the St. Lawrence Estuary.

Most of my work starts close to home - coastal walks with my husky Apollo, hikes up the surrounding peaks - and extends to city breaks and further travels.

I often shoot with a minimalist kit built around prime lenses, favoring a deliberate, unhurried approach to composition.

My photography is how I try to share the way I experience moments and places.

Thanks for stopping by, and feel free to get in touch if any of this resonates.
